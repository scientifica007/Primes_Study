## 3. Quotient-generator column upper bound (application to our model)

Write G for units, A=M\(G union K), a=|A|. Define t as the least size of S subset A such that every nonzero quotient element is a positive word in images of G union S. The quotient and map are supplied, so S and one word for each exterior label y can be computed before any query. All unit-factor products are already known (nonzero quotient or K-products). For each s in S query only the unknown cells (x,s) with x in A; at most a*t. For any exterior y, evaluate x times its known quotient word by successive right multiplication by letters. The lifted word evaluates to y since its quotient output is nonzero. If an intermediate value enters K, all subsequent transitions are known. Thus the entire table, and consequently labelled E, can be recovered nonadaptively. No labelled ideals or rank required for this upper algorithm. The dual uses generator rows. With stronger metadata skip every supplied entry as well. Minimize the number of unknown cells over feasible S, not necessarily minimum cardinality. If r is the positive-word rank, t<=r (a generating set restricted to exterior nonunits together with G generates all nonzero quotient elements). In particular a*t=o(n²) when t=o(n), and the existing decision/count bound may be minimized with this. No new universal coefficient below 1/16 for the decision problem. This is a straightforward application of associativity and known generators, related to ZMR's reconstruction principles and Chang's Lemma 26, not a claim of novelty for generator-column recovery.

Executed [quotient audit](../experiments/quotient-generator-column-audit.js): 148 tables in 11 existing families, 78,712 recovered cells including 16,576 evaluations of nontrivial quotient words (length >1), 2,261,000 associativity triples, PASS. The script uses proper-principal fixed tables too, and a greedy inclusion-minimal S, not guaranteed cardinality-minimum; it verifies the stated bound for its chosen S.

## 4. Sharp full-table learning coefficient 1 even with complete generating statistics

Strengthen the previous anchored Chang adaptation by dropping commutativity and using a fixed two-element ideal. For n>=5 let M={e,z,t} disjoint union V, |V|=n-3. Choose two distinct anchors a,b in V and let U=V\{a,b}, |U|=n-5. Set K={z,t}. Identity e acts identically. For all nonidentity pairs set the product z except:
- ab=ba=t;
- bu=ub=t for u in U;
- for each ordered pair (u,v) of DISTINCT elements of U, independently assign uv either z or t.

All squares in V are z; a*u=u*a=z. Every product of three nonidentity elements is z, proving associativity. The only unit is e; all nonidentity powers stabilize at z. Every high label x in V has left and right principal ideal {x,z,t}; the anchors ensure both target values occur, including for a,b. The ideals of t and z are K and {z}; those of e are M. All two-sided principal ideals coincide with the corresponding one-sided ideals. Thus all labelled ideals, quotient, products with K, unit actions, regularity and idempotence are fixed. The only variable cells involve distinct high labels, which never lie together in any proper principal ideal, so its internal table is fixed too.

Every generating set must contain e and all V because none of these labels is a product of two other labels. They generate z by a² and t by ab. Hence
\[
E=\{e\}\cup V,\quad r=n-2,\quad g_r=1,\quad H(w)=w^{n-2},\quad G_{\rm ord}(w)=w^{n-2}(1+w)^2.
\]
Even on the full compatible fibre E is forced: the quotient excludes production of exterior labels other than through e, the fixed K-products determine z, and the high principal ideals force a nonself production of t. On the displayed subfamily, each ordered free cell has an independent bit. Exact full-table recovery must query all (n-5)(n-6) free cells, since changing one unqueried bit leaves all other replies and supplied data unchanged; querying them suffices on the promise. Hence the worst-case exact deterministic table-recovery cost in either P03 model, even with the whole ordinary and irredundant generating enumerators, satisfies
\[
(n-5)(n-6)\le D_{\rm learn}(n)\le n^2,\qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}D_{\rm learn}(n)/n^2=1.
\]
This holds for adaptive and nonadaptive deterministic learning. Meanwhile E recovery in this fibre is zero-query. The global worst-case decision/count coefficient remains 1/16 from the manuscript. Do not conflate a zero-query fibre with the global optimum. This construction is an elementary directed variant inspired by Chang; do not claim historical priority without further checking. Unlike the earlier symmetric version its free entries are ordered and K has constant size.

Executed [directed audit](../experiments/directed-table-learning-separation-audit.js): 4,210 tables, 3,096,497 associativity triples, 342,465 reconstructed cells, 608 direct subset-closure classifications, 62 single-cell-flip checks; PASS. n=5,6,7,8,9 exhaustive free patterns (1,1,4,64,4096); n=12 only 44 selected patterns, not all 2^42. The generator-word checks here are length one; the separate quotient audit exercises longer words. No formal proof certification.
